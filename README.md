# Nina Brenes

### Creative AI Operator · Marketing & Operations · Implementation

I turn messy ideas into clear systems, useful prototypes, and better ways of working.

My background is in marketing, strategy, and creative direction. Today, I work at the intersection of AI, operations, and implementation — helping people and teams move from “we should use AI” to something concrete, thoughtful, and actually usable.

I’m especially interested in the space between strategy and execution:

- translating business needs into practical AI workflows
- designing systems that make teams faster and more organized
- turning research into decisions, briefs, dashboards, and action
- prototyping tools, automations, and internal experiences
- helping teams adopt new ways of working
- bringing structure to creative and marketing operations

I’m not interested in building technology for its own sake.

I care about building the right thing, making it understandable, and helping people use it with confidence.

## How I like to work

I start with the real problem, not the tool.

Then I look for the smallest useful version we can build, test, and improve.

My process usually combines:

1. understanding the people and workflow involved
2. clarifying the decision or outcome we want
3. mapping the system behind the work
4. prototyping a practical solution
5. documenting it clearly
6. helping the team make it part of how they work
